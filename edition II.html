Showdowns at FinFlare 2026

CLASSES 11–12

MockStreet

Classes 11 to 12

MockStreet is a stimulation of the real time stock market. Create portfolios, place trades, and track gains by experiencing the ups and downs of investing, all without risking actual cash.

PowerPitch

Classes 11 to 12

PowerPitch is a dynamic pitch deck competition where bold ideas meet real opportunity. Craft compelling business stories, pitch to expert panels, and compete to turn the startup dreams into reality.

BoardroomBattle

Classes 11 to 12

BoardroomBattle throws students into the hot seat of corporate leaders. Tackle rapid-fire quizzes, crisis simulations, and boardroom grilling before delivering a high-stakes final pitch.

CLASSES 9–10

CaseCipher

Classes 9 to 10

CaseCipher challenges students to crack real-world business crises through strategic thinking. Dive into management dilemmas, craft winning solutions, and outsmart the competition with every case.

BrandCraft

Classes 9 to 10

BrandCraft is a creative showdown where young minds design striking ads and pitches for real-world brands or causes. Blend marketing genius with storytelling and sharp commercial instinct.

BudgetBreaker

Classes 9 to 10

BudgetBreaker is a budgeting challenge where teams balance expenses against limited resources. Draft smart allocations, justify every rupee, and pitch your budget to the judges.

CLASSES 6–8

QuizNomics

Classes 6 to 8

QuizNomics is a fast-paced quiz where sharp minds tackle questions on economics, finance, and business. Compete, calculate, and conquer as you test your money smarts under pressure.

MonopolyMasters

Classes 6 to 8

MonopolyMasters brings the classic board game to life in a fast-paced contest of wealth and wit. Outplay opponents in timed rounds and rise as the ultimate tycoon of the Monopoly table.

EchoSpark

Classes 6 to 8

EchoSpark is a thought-provoking debate where bold voices tackle real-world finance issues. Spark dialogue, challenge ideas, and echo solutions because purpose deserves a platform.

CLASSES 3–5

FinTales

Classes 3 to 5

FinTales is a creative story writing competition where imagination meets money sense. Weave engaging narratives that spark financial awareness and inspire smart choices for all.

FinStrokes

Classes 3 to 5

FinStrokes is a vibrant art competition where creativity meets commerce. Promote awareness and make money matters speak louder through impactful sketches and colours.

Junior events (FinTales, FinStrokes) happen only at your school. Top 3 per school win; overall top 3 are felicitated at the Finale. No travel needed.

What's in FinFlare for you?

  • Exciting prizes and medals for winners and certificates signed by renowned financial experts for all participants.
  • Top three entrepreneurial ideas will receive opportunity for startup incubation and potential funding from industry leaders.
  • Internship opportunities with the FinFlare team to kickstart career goals.
  • Opportunity to receive personalised letters of recommendation highlighting individual prowess.
  • Social media shoutout to outstanding performers.
  • Direct exposure to a wide network of professionals including CAs, CFAs, MBAs from globally recognised organisations for mentorship and career insights.
  • Develop key skills across personal and professional domains like communication, collaboration, analytical thinking, strategy, and decision-making.

Recognition in FinFlare

The FinFlare Award – Winner School

The school with the highest score grading will win the FinFlare Award!

Grading System:

  • Winning teams for each event will score 10 points for their school
  • 1st Runner up teams for each event will score 8 points for their school
  • 2nd Runner up teams for each event will score 6 points for their school
  • Other Finalist teams for each event will score 2 points for their school

The FinFlare Prime Award – School with Most Participants

The school with most number of participants in the Qualifier Round will win the Prime Award!

The ShowDown Awards – Event Winners and Runners Ups

Everyone deserves a FinFlare but there are some who would outshine the others!

  • Winners for each event
  • 1st Runner Up for each event
  • 2nd Runner Up for each event
