About FinFlare

The world demands more than just having a job. Finance and taxes are no longer distant affairs of ledgers and accountants; they have become the quiet disciplines of survival. The art of planning, saving and investing is now the difference between drifting and standing steady.

In a modern digital world where everything stands at a disposal of a few finger-clicks, money and its management has become evermore important, and the susceptibility towards financial frauds has increased.

FinFlare is an initiative which aims to make financial literacy accessible to our younger generations; such that they can take more informed decisions as well as become more self-dependent for understanding as well as taking control over their personal finances. This youth-led endeavour encourages basic finance and its management to be a skill everyone possesses before they begin earning!
